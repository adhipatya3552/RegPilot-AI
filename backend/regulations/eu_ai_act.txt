EU AI Act 2024
- AI systems classified as: Unacceptable, High Risk, Limited Risk, Minimal Risk
- High risk AI (hiring, credit, healthcare) requires conformity assessment
- Must maintain logs and audit trails for high risk AI
- Transparency required: users must know they are interacting with AI
- Prohibited: social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance
- General Purpose AI models must publish training data summary
- Fines up to €35M or 7% of global revenue
- Human oversight required for high risk systems
- Bias testing and fairness documentation required
