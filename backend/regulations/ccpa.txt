CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) - USA
- Users can opt out of sale of personal data
- Right to know what data is collected and why
- Right to delete personal data on request
- Must display "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link
- Cannot discriminate against users who exercise privacy rights
- Applies to businesses with $25M+ revenue or 50K+ users data
- Annual data inventory and mapping required
- Privacy policy must be updated every 12 months
- Fines up to $7,500 per intentional violation
