SOC2 (Service Organization Control 2) - USA
- Five Trust Service Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy
- Must implement access controls and multi-factor authentication
- Encryption required for data at rest and in transit
- Incident response plan must be documented and tested
- Vendor risk management program required
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability scanning
- Change management procedures must be documented
- Audit logs must be maintained and reviewed
- Annual third-party audit required for Type II certification
- Employee security training required
